GSoC 2026 — JdeRobot

Hi, I’m Kartik Jangid

I’m a second-year Computer Science student at the Scaler School of Technology (in collaboration with BITS Pilani), based in Bengaluru, India. I’m a GSoC 2026 contributor with JdeRobot, working on Project #7: optimizing the RoboticsBackend Docker infrastructure and migrating to rootless Podman.

This blog documents my work week by week — the architecture decisions, the debugging rabbit holes, and the actual commits that move the project forward.

What I’m building: Restructuring the monolithic dependencies_humble Docker image into a lean multi-stage build, pruning heavy ROS 2 meta-packages (Aerostack2, OMPL), and migrating the runtime to rootless Podman with CDI-based GPU acceleration.

My mentors for this project are David Pascual-Hernández, Javier I., Md. Shariar Kabir, and Nikhil Gupta.

Outside of this project, I’m researching humanoid robotics (co-authoring a survey paper on data acquisition regimes for Elsevier) and building an offline ML pipeline that classifies badminton strokes using pose extraction — I train daily and compete seriously, so it’s a project I actually care about.

Feel free to reach out via LinkedIn or follow the project on GitHub.
